# EvidenceOps Agent Brand Guidelines

## Positioning

EvidenceOps Agent is an explainable AI incident response workspace for analysts who need speed without losing evidence integrity.

## Promise

Evidence that moves at machine speed and still stands up in review.

## Visual System

- Forensic Ink: `#111316`
- Case Paper: `#F7F6F1`
- Evidence Red: `#D43D2F`
- Signal Blue: `#2367A5`
- Approval Green: `#1F7A55`
- Secondary Amber: `#B77416`

Use high-contrast layouts, evidence tags, grid lines, and report-like structure. Avoid glossy AI gradients, decorative purple effects, and magical language.

## Logo Usage

- Primary dark lockup: use on light backgrounds.
- Primary light lockup: use on dark backgrounds.
- Symbol: use for app icon, favicon, avatar, and compact placements.
- Keep clear space equal to the width of the symbol's red evidence block.

## Voice

EvidenceOps sounds calm, precise, and review-ready.

Say:

- "Likely exfiltration; external destination not yet proven."
- "Approval required before token revocation."
- "Finding cites EV-001 and EV-004."

Avoid:

- "The AI solved the incident."
- "Autonomous containment without review."
- "Guaranteed breach prevention."

## Product Story

EvidenceOps correlates identity, OAuth, endpoint, storage, and observability signals into a defensible incident packet. The product emphasizes evidence IDs, tool traces, confidence, uncertainty, and human approval gates.
