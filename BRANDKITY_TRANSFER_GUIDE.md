## BrandKit Name

## Tagline

Evidence that moves at machine speed and still stands up in review.

## Short Description

EvidenceOps Agent is an explainable AI incident response workspace for analysts who need fast triage without losing evidence integrity. The brand system combines forensic report structure, evidence IDs, terminal traces, and human approval gates.

## Brand Story

AI incident response tools often sound magical, but security teams need defensible evidence. EvidenceOps Agent turns identity, OAuth, endpoint, storage, and observability signals into a traceable incident packet. The brand therefore behaves like a case file: precise, calm, high-contrast, and review-ready.

## Typography

- Headlines: Georgia / Instrument Serif style.
- Labels and audit trails: Courier New / IBM Plex Mono style.
- Tone: senior analyst, not magical AI.

## Voice Rules

Say:

- "Likely exfiltration; external destination not yet proven."
- "Approval required before token revocation."
- "Finding cites EV-001 and EV-004."

Avoid:

- "The AI solved the incident."
- "Autonomous containment without review."
- "Guaranteed breach prevention."
